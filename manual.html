Telltale Debrief

How to use the page: the workflow, then what the mouse and the keys do.

Debrief reads GRATIA's Expedition log and turns it into a debrief: findings, figures and a PDF. It runs in this browser tab. Your files are read here and are never uploaded. The one exception is Fetch wind map: it asks Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap for the weather and the coastline of the race area on that day, and sends nothing of the log.

It needs a browser with WebGPU: Chrome, Edge, or Safari 18 or later. Setup and Sailing are in the desktop app. On Windows and Linux, read Ctrl wherever this says Cmd.

The workflow

1. Open the logs

  • One log: click Load log… and pick the Expedition .csv. The page makes a regatta file for it, regatta.toml, holding what the log proposes: a race from each countdown, the start line's ends from the pings. Each proposal is shown with a "?" until you accept it.
  • A whole regatta: click Open regatta… and pick the folder holding regatta.toml and the day logs, with its wind folder if the desktop app fetched one. The last day opens. The days are listed under the regatta file; click one to open its log. Load log… adds another day to the regatta file that is open.

The regatta file names each day's log by its name in the folder. A log you add with Load log… belongs in that folder: copy it there on your computer too, or the next Open regatta… lists its day but cannot open it.

Times are in the regatta file's zone when it names one (zone = "Europe/Paris"), otherwise in this computer's. The side panel says which.

2. Set up each race

Click a race under its day, or press its number. Under it:

  • gun and finish: a "?" means the log proposed it. Confirm accepts it. Or type the time as digits, 122002 for 12:20:02, and press Enter; Esc drops what you typed.
  • name: type and press Enter. × deletes the race, after Yes.
  • Start replays the start; each Leg link replays that leg.
  • Course: Confirm the marks and line ends the log proposed, or place them. Pin the cursor where she rounded or where the end was, then click Mark at the boat, Port end at the boat or Starboard end at the boat. Gate… and Finish line… take typed positions, like 43 15.120 N 6 42.960 E.
  • New race from the range: drag a range on the strip first; the race runs from its start to its end.
  • Notes, Calibration changes and Sails fold open to list what the day holds, with Note at the cursor…, Calibration change at the cursor… and Sail for the range… to add one.

3. Read the pages

Overview, Speed, Start and course, Tacks and gybes, Wind, Instruments and Regatta. Each reads the range chosen on the strip: a race, a leg, or any stretch you drag. Regatta reads every day of the regatta file and compares the races.

4. The wind map

Click Fetch wind map above the map, once per day, then Model wind in the map's legend. The model's wind is drawn under the track. The coastline comes from OpenStreetMap's server, which is often busy: when it does not answer, the side panel says so and the button stays, so click it again a few minutes later. What is fetched stays in the tab until it closes.

5. Export

Export PDF… saves every page over the range. Set-up sheet… saves the navigator's set-up sheet: what the log shows of how Expedition was set up, and the steps that fix each fault. Both go to your Downloads folder.

6. Save the regatta file

Edits are kept in this tab only. Save regatta file downloads regatta.toml; put it in the regatta's folder in place of the old one. Until you do, the side panel says "Edits not saved: they are in this page alone.", and the browser asks before the tab closes. Opening the same folder again keeps the page's edited regatta file rather than the folder's older copy.

Mouse

The strip, across the top

Hover
The cursor follows the pointer on every view. Its label gives the clock and the time since the gun.
Click
Pins the cursor there. On a leg in the legs row, or a turn in the turns row, it replays that leg or turn.
Drag across
Sets the range.
Drag a △ at either end of the bottom row
Moves that end of the range.
Drag a race's gun or finish line
Moves it; where you let go is written to the regatta file.
Double-click
The whole log.
Scroll
Moves the window earlier or later.
Pinch, or Cmd + scroll
Zooms about the pointer.
+ and ×
+ adds a lane (type in its box to filter the channels); × removes one. Drag the strip's bottom edge to give the lanes more or less height.
▶ and 10×
▶ plays the range and ⏸ pauses it; 10× sets the speed, from 1× to 60×.
Hover an event, leg, turn or sail
Says what it is.

The map

Hover near the track
The cursor goes to that second.
Click
Pins the cursor. On a tack's or gybe's mark, replays that turn.
Drag, or scroll
Pans.
Pinch, or Cmd + scroll
Zooms about the pointer.
Double-click
Fits the map to the range.
Up:
Which way is up: wind, north, start line or course axis.
Legend buttons
What colours the track: Speed, Wind direction, % of target, VMC, Tactical, Leg, Model wind.
Measure
Then two clicks on the map: the distance between them in metres and nautical miles, and the true bearing from the first to the second.
Edit course
Drag the start line's ends and the marks; where one is let go is written to the regatta file. A press anywhere else pans.

The figures on a page

Hover
The value under the pointer; on a time axis the cursor follows.
Click
Pins the cursor. On a bar of a ranking of turns, picks that turn out wherever it is drawn. On a race's dot on Regatta, makes that race the range, opening its day's log if needed.
Drag
Pans.
Pinch, or Cmd + scroll
Zooms about the pointer.
Double-click
Shows it whole again.
Scroll
Scrolls the page.

Keys

Click anywhere on the page first so that it has the keyboard. While you type in a box the keys are the box's.

← →
Move the pinned cursor one second. The first press pins it at the start of the range.
Cmd + ← →
Ten seconds.
Space
Play and pause.
Esc
Stops playback and unpins the cursor.
1 to 9
That race of the day.
0
The whole log.
